Support

Help, troubleshooting, and contact

Entangle is a free, open-source remote that turns your iPhone into a pocket trackpad and keyboard for any Mac on your local network. There are no accounts and no cloud — your phone connects directly to your Mac over Wi-Fi.

Getting started

  • Install the Entangle desktop app on your Mac and launch it. The desktop app advertises itself on your local network.
  • Grant macOS Accessibility permission to the Entangle desktop app when prompted. This is required so Entangle can move the cursor and synthesize keystrokes on your Mac.
  • On your iPhone, open Entangle. The app will discover your Mac automatically. Tap your Mac's name to connect.
  • Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Troubleshooting

  • My phone can't find my Mac. Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and that the desktop app is running. On iOS, also make sure Entangle has Local Network permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Some routers (and "guest" or AP-isolated networks) block Bonjour discovery — try a different network.
  • I'm connected but the cursor doesn't move. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility on your Mac and confirm Entangle is enabled. The desktop app will also surface a permission prompt on first launch.
  • The connection drops or feels laggy. Make sure you're on a 5 GHz Wi-Fi network. Crowded 2.4 GHz networks can add noticeable latency.
  • Keyboard or modifier keys aren't working as expected. Force-quit and relaunch the desktop app to reset its input state.
